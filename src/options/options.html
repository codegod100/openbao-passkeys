OpenBao connection

Passkeys and passwords are written to a KV v2 mount. Use token, AppRole, or OIDC to authenticate.

Register this redirect URI on the OpenBao OIDC role (allowed_redirect_uris) and your IdP:

Prefer OpenBao callback_mode=direct or device if your IdP rejects chrome-extension:// redirect URIs. For direct mode, set the redirect override to <openbao-url>/v1/auth/<mount>/oidc/callback.

OpenBao path layout

Passkeys: <mount>/data/<passkeys-prefix>/<credentialId>
Passwords: <mount>/data/<passwords-prefix>/<id>
